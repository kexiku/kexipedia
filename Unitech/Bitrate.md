*Bitrate* is the number of bits used to represent one second of a digital [[audio]].

It is measured in kilobits per second (kbps) and is calculated by multiplying the [[bit depth]] by the [[sample rate]], then by the number of audio [[channels]].

For example, a stereo 16-bit audio with a 44.1 kHz sample rate would have a bitrate of 1,411,200 bps, or 1411.2 kbps (16 × 44100 × 2).
