## ❔ Conditional expressions

- `-eq`: Equal to
- `-ne`: Not equal to
- `-lt`: Less than
- `-le`: Less than or equal to
- `-gt`: Greater than
- `-ge`: Greater than or equal to

## 🔢 Arithmetic evaluation

- `==`: Equal to
- `!=`: Not equal to
- `<`: Less than
- `<=`: Less than or equal to
- `>`: Greater than
- `>=`: Greater than or equal to
