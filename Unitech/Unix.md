A multiuser modular operating system based on Multics

- First written in assembly for 18-bit [PDP-7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDP-7) minicomputer
  (was rewritten in [[C]] in 1973)
- Has influenced many modern systems such as Linux, BSD, and macOS
- The key principles of Unix formed the set of cultural norms and development approaches known as *[[Unix philosophy]]*
