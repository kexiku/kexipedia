A *terminal* is a text-based input and output environment.

It passes entered commands to the [[shell]] and dispays their ouput to the screen.

A *terminal window*, also known as a *terminal emulator*, is a software application that emulates a [[console]] in a [[User interfaces|graphical user interface]].

Examples of common [[Linux]] terminal emulators include [Gnome terminal](https://help.gnome.org/gnome-terminal/), [Kitty](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/) and [Alacritty](https://alacritty.org/).
