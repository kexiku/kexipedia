*Encoding* is the process of converting sound into a digital format that a computer can store, process, or transmit.

Audio codecs used for encoding can be either *lossy* ([[MP3]], [[OPUS]]) or *lossless* ([[FLAC]], [[ALAC]], [[WAV]]).

Lossy encoding removes significant amount of data from the [[PCM]] stream to save space, while lossless encoding transforms the PCM stream in such a way that no quality is lost.
