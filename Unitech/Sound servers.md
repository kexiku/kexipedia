Modern [[Linux]] distributions use *sound servers* to handle [[audio]] streams.

They act as an intermediary layer between user applications and the low-level [[kernel]] audio driver (either [[ALSA]] or [[OSS]]).

Sound servers manage complex tasks like mixing audio from multiple apps, handling volume control per application, and routing audio dynamically.

Examples of Linux sound servers include [[PulseAudio]], [[JACK]] and [[PipeWire]].
