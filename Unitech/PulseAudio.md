*PulseAudio* is a general purpose [[Sound servers|sound server]] for [[Linux]] that acts as a middleware between user applications and the [[kernel]] audio driver.

It works on top of [[ALSA]], taking control of its single audio stream and forcing other applications to talk to PulseAudio instead.

While PulseAudio has historically been the standard sound server for Linux, many modern distributions have transitioned to [[PipeWire]], which provides built-in PulseAudio compatibility.
