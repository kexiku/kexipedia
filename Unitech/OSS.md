The *Open Sound System* (*OSS*) was the original [[kernel]]-level sound interface in [[Linux]] and [[UNIX]]-like operation systems.

It is based on standard Unix device [[system calls]].

It was superseded by the [[ALSA]], which has a lot of of advantages over OSS:

- Better support for USB audio devices
- Support for Bluetooth audio devices
- Better support for MIDI devices
- Better support for jack detection
