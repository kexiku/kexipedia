*DeFi lending* is an automated mechanism that allows people to lend their digital assets to others and earn interest without using traditional bank systems.

[[Smart contracts]] act as intermediaries that guarantee the security of lending for all participants in DeFi.

## 💸 How it works

- Lenders drop assets into a shared [[Liquidity pools|pool]] to earn interest
- Borrowers take assets from the pool by locking up other assets as a security deposit called *collateral*

The key principle underlying the DeFi lending concept is that the borrower must lock up collateral worth *more* than the amount they borrow.
