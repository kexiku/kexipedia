A *cryptographic key* is a string of characters used by an algorithm to encrypt or decrypt data.

In [[blockchain]], keys are crucial to provide secure digital transactions

Keys can either be *public* or *private* depending on their visibility.

## 👥 Public keys

- Usable for receiving funds
- Can be safely shared with anyone

## 👤 Private keys

- Acts like a digital signature
- Must be kept confidential
- Proves ownership of assets

If you lose your private keys, you lose access to your money.

> [!danger] Remember: not your keys, not your coins.
