A *decentralized exchange* (*DEX*) is a peer-to-peer marketplace that lets users swap crypto assets directly using [[smart contracts]].

While DEXs and centralized exchanges both allow users to swap assets, they operate in fundamentally different ways:

- 🏦 **Centralized exchanges** (**CEX**) operate similarly to brokerages or banks by holding your funds, curating which assets can be traded and complying with government regulations
    
- 🦄 **Decentralized exchanges** offer much more control and autonomy, allowing users to swap without an intermediary thanks to smart contracts
    
Using DEX, you retain full control of your private keys.

The exchange doesn't usually provide a holding mechanism for your funds - instead, you must connect an external personal wallet and approve transactions directly from your device.

That ensures a very high security standards: no one can freeze your assets or lock your account just because some restricting amendment to the legislation was signed - your funds is always in your pocket.

While being a direct part of a DeFi system, DEXs often offer a wide range of experimental, early-stage, or niche tokens for swapping
