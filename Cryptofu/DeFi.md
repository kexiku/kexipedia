*DeFi* (short for *decentralized finance*) is a global digital financial system that operates on public [[Blockchain|blockchains]].

Unlike traditional finance systems, there are no middlemen like banks or governments in DeFi - instead, users rely on [[smart contracts]] as intermediaries for making peer-to-peer transactions.

DeFi services are open to anyone regardless of nationality, age or credit history - no one has the right to block access to your decentralized funds.

## Main advantages of DeFi

- 🛡️ Full control over your money
- 🔒 Transactions are pseudonymous
- ⚡ Transfers takes minutes (or even seconds)

## 💸 Ways to use DeFi

Users typically engage with DeFi via software called [[dApps]], most of which are run on the [[Ethereum]] blockchain.

With dApps, you can do a lot of actions with your assets such as [[trading]], getting loans, [[lending]] tokens to earn interest, and much more.
