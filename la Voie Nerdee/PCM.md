To represent an analog audio in a digital format, modern computers use method called *PCM* (stands for *Pulse-Code Modulation*).

For PCM to be converted into an analog signal, you need a *DAC* (*Digital to Analog Converter*), which is usually performed by a sound card.

A PCM stream has two basic properties: the [[sample rate]] and the [[bit depth]].
