## 📑 Recommended properties ordering flow

1. Positioning & Context: `position`, `top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`, `z-index`
2. Display & Flex/Grid (box outer): `display`, `flex-direction`, `justify-content`, `align-items`, `gap`
3. Dimensions & Margins: `width`, `height`, `margin`
4. Padding & Borders (box inner): `padding`, `border`, `border-radius`, `overflow`
5. Typography & Text: `font-family`, `font-size`, `font-weight`, `line-height`, `text-align`
6. Visuals & Decoration: `color`, `background`, `box-shadow`, `opacity`
7. Transitions & Misc: `transition`, `transform`, `cursor`
