*Dynamic range* in audio is the difference in decibels (dB) between the quietest and loudest parts of a sound.

A *wide* dynamic range means there is a massive contrast between soft, whispering details and explosive, loud peaks (common in classical music or jazz).

A *narrow* dynamic range means the volume stays relatively flat and consistent (common in modern pop, rock, or EDM).

> [!note]
> The natural dynamic range of human hearing spans roughly 90 to 120 dB, ranging from a soft 30 dB whisper to a 120 dB pain threshold
