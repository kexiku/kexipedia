*Audio frequency* (*AF*) is the rate at which a sound wave vibrates per second, which determines the *pitch* of a sound.

Audio frequency measured in Hertz (Hz), where 1 Hz means one complete wave cycle per second.

> [!note]
> The average human ear can hear frequencies from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz (20 kHz)

Waves below 20 Hz are called *infrasound*, and waves above 20 kHz are called *ultrasound*.
